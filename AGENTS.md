# Project workflow

## Progress reports and writing

- Put progress reports in a dated subdirectory of `docs/progress/` and update its Chinese and English indexes.
- Provide each new main report in Chinese and English. Deliver both Markdown and PDF; keep the LaTeX source used to compile each PDF alongside the report.
- Write fluent, direct, professional prose in both languages. Explain terms, figures, methods, and context in plain language for readers who do not know the code. Use established terminology and concrete facts; avoid invented labels, jargon, canned AI phrasing, defensive language, repetitive caveats, and generic warnings.
- Identify the data and evaluation set when presenting results. Explain limitations only when they materially affect interpretation, briefly and in context. Keep methods and results accurate and distinguish selected checkpoints from final epochs.
- Review both languages and all report figures and PDF pages before delivery. Preserve historical reports unless the user asks to revise them.
- When moving reports, update their indexes and all references. Preserve paths used by training and data pipelines.
